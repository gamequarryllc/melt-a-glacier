Melt a Glacier: support

Help with the game on iPhone and Android

Contact

Email gamequarryllc@protonmail.com. Tell us what phone you have and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

I bought Remove Ads or the Golden Hair Dryer and it's gone. Open the Catalogue (the button at the top right) and tap Restore purchases. You need to be signed in to the same Apple or Google account you bought it with.

I'm stuck in the ice. Tap Respawn at the top left. It puts you back at the start without losing anything.

Where is my progress saved? On your device only. Deleting the game deletes your save.

How do I change my ad choices? In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, open the Catalogue and tap Ad privacy choices. On iPhone you can also change tracking under Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Refunds. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. Ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com or in Google Play under your order history.
