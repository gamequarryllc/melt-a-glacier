Privacy policy

Melt a Glacier · last updated 9 November 2026

This policy covers the game "Melt a Glacier" on iPhone, iPad and Android. In short: the game has no accounts and keeps your progress on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what the ad network needs to show ads and what the app stores need to sell you something.

What stays on your device

Your save game (how much ice you have melted, your tools, finds and settings) is stored only on your device. We don't receive it, and deleting the game deletes it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: video ads you choose to watch for a reward, and occasional full-screen ads. To pick and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent first. You can change your choice any time under Catalogue → Ad privacy choices in the game.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the game asks permission before using your advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency). If you say no, you still get ads, just less personalised ones.
  • Buying Remove Ads turns off the full-screen ads.

Google's own policy explains how it uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases (Apple, Google and RevenueCat)

Payments are handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check purchases and restore them on a new device. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous ID created by the app, your purchase history for this game, and basic device information. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account.
  • We don't sell your personal data.
  • The game isn't aimed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your phone's settings, change your ad consent in the game, and delete all game data by uninstalling the game. For data held by Google or RevenueCat, use the links above or contact us and we'll help.
